title: Exit and entry servers
description: 'Plain servers the panel reaches over SSH once. An exit (role
  "exit", the default) sits in another country: WireGuard and a single
  forwarding rule go on it, and a cisco inbound can send one of its countries
  out through it. An entry server (role "front") works the other way round:
  customers connect to its address, and the panel’s own inbounds reach it
  through a Backhaul tunnel that the panel dials. Nothing else is installed on
  either: no panel and no accounts. Super admin only. All endpoints under
  /panel/api/relays.'
